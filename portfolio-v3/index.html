01 Predictive modelling · Public infrastructure

Water Main Break Prediction

Exploring how machine learning can help identify infrastructure at risk in Kitchener–Waterloo.

Inside the project

The question

Can historical water main data help anticipate break frequency and identify areas that deserve a closer look?

The approach

A workflow around data preprocessing, feature engineering, and a Random Forest regression model, with MLflow for evaluation and a Streamlit interface for exploring predictions.

Why it matters

It connects a modelling problem to a practical municipal decision: where to focus attention before a disruption happens.

Python · Scikit-learn · MLflow · Streamlit

02 Customer research · Retail

Retail Customer Behaviour

Using segmentation and uplift modelling to turn transaction data into store strategy.

Inside the project

The question

What do purchase patterns reveal about customers, and how can we evaluate a change to a store?

The approach

During my Quantium internship, I analysed customer transactions, used K-means segmentation to examine purchasing behaviour, and applied uplift modelling to identify benchmark stores for evaluating trial layouts.

Why it matters

A useful analysis connects what people buy with a clear commercial recommendation and a way to evaluate its impact.

Python · Pandas · K-means · Uplift modelling

03 Segmentation · Predictive modelling

Driver Lifetime Value

Investigating the behaviours and driver segments associated with long-term value.

Inside the project

The question

Which factors are associated with a driver's lifetime value, and do distinct groups of drivers generate different levels of value?

The approach

Exploration of driver data using Python and visual analysis, applying K-means clustering to examine segments and investigate the factors behind lifetime value.

Why it matters

Understanding differences between segments creates a basis for more focused recommendations about driver relationships.

Pandas · Seaborn · K-means

04 Customer research · Data engineering

Market Basket Analysis

Finding relationships between purchases in the Instacart grocery dataset.

Inside the project

The question

Which products are frequently purchased together, and how might those relationships inform recommendations?

The approach

Databricks, Spark, and Spark SQL to ingest and explore the Instacart dataset, then PySpark and Scala to identify frequent itemsets.

Why it matters

Basket-level patterns offer a different view of customer behaviour: the relationships between products, rather than individual sales alone.

Databricks · Apache Spark · SQL · Scala

05 Predictive modelling · Customer experience

Delivery Duration Prediction

Modelling DoorDash delivery times to explore a more reliable customer expectation.

Inside the project

The question

How accurately can we estimate the time between placing an order and receiving a delivery?

The approach

Using a dataset of 2015 DoorDash deliveries, I explored classical regression and boosted tree models, including LightGBM, to predict delivery duration.

Why it matters

The prediction is part of a customer experience. A useful model helps set a realistic expectation at the moment an order is placed.

Statsmodels · PCA · LightGBM
